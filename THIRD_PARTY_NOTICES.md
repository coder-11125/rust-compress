## AV1 (AVIF): `src/avif/av1/cdf_tables.rs`, `coef_tables.rs`, `qm_tables.rs`, quantizer lookups

The AV1 bitstream specification (Alliance for Open Media) defines default symbol CDFs, coefficient scan orders,
context offset tables, the DC/AC quantizer lookup tables and the quantizer matrices. The values in this crate were
generated by scripts from the corresponding tables in **rav1e** (<https://github.com/xiph/rav1e>) and **rav1d**
(<https://github.com/memorysafety/rav1d>, a port of dav1d), both distributed under the BSD 2-Clause licence, and
were then re-laid-out (for example transposed to the specification's row-major conventions). The decoder was
checked bit for bit against dav1d on streams from several independent encoders.
